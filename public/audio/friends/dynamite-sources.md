# Dynamite sound sources

- `dynamite_explosion_last.mp3`: **Explosive.flac**, uploaded by **qubodup**. Actual explosive-device detonation, extracted from US government footage according to the uploader. CC0/public domain. Source: https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/189778/ . Public HQ preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/189/189778_71257-hq.mp3 . Trimmed to the third and final explosion: 4.10 seconds through the end of the decoded preview (8.478 seconds), preserving its decay with short edge fades. No pitch shifting or synthetic layering. A new filename prevents cached copies of the original three-explosion recording from playing.
- `dynamite_fuse.ogg`: **Simple Fuse Sound**, by **themightyglider**, derived from **beefirecracker.wav** by **j1987**, a recording of a firework fuse. Both sources CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/simple-fuse-sound . Original recording: https://freesound.org/people/j1987/sounds/111325/ . Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fuse_0.ogg . Repeated for the three-second fuse and stopped at detonation.

Retrieved October 10, 2026. The previously generated explosion WAV and edited firecracker explosion are no longer used.
