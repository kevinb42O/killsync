# Real fishing recordings

Bundled October 10, 2026. All three sources are CC0. Files are downloaded and
served locally; gameplay never contacts the sound libraries. `fishing-sources.json`
records source and exported SHA-256 hashes, exact trims and processing.

The cast source is a fly rod rather than the game's bobber rig, and the landing
source is a small stone rather than bait. They provide authentic short line
movement and a small-object water impact; neither is labelled as an exact recording
of this rig. No generated audio, composite casts, pitch shifting, catch chimes,
or repetitive bite alarms. The reel plays one continuous excerpt while reeling,
and stops on cancellation, tool changes or disposal. Casting and landing trigger
once each; a bite uses one quieter shortened water excerpt, while its repeated
visual ripples remain silent.
