# Stillwater retreat materials

Real Poly Haven PBR maps, downloaded 9 October 2026 and bundled locally. All
assets are CC0-1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license. Runtime does not contact Poly
Haven. `manifest.json` records the official download URLs, authors, physical
scale metadata, map resolutions and MD5 checksums. Downloads were verified
against the provider's checksums. Files are unmodified.

| Asset | Use | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White Plaster 02 | Warm ivory walls/ceiling | https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02 |
| Wood Floor | Oak floor | https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor |
| Wood Plank Wall | Exterior, trim, benches | https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_plank_wall |
| Poly Wool Herringbone | Muted moss couch | https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone |

Colour is 2K for plaster/floor and 1K for boards/fabric. NormalGL and roughness
maps are 1K. Colour maps use sRGB; data maps are linear. UV scales in the renderer
are expressed in the simulation's 12 units/metre. Tint is applied in materials,
with separate bounded warm lighting and window illumination inside the room.
