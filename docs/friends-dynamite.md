# Dynamite in Friends Fun mode

Open the Fun bar with T and select slot 5 (Dynamite). An unlit bundle appears in your right hand. Click or press the controller fire button to light and throw it in the direction you are looking. Each charge burns for three seconds, bounces off walls, and settles on the ground. The hand performs a throw gesture and refills with another bundle. Teammates also see the held bundle and thrown charge.

The explosion destroys player-made structures and natural terrain within 112 world units. Soil, stone, copper and iron all use the same terrain edit path; every construction shape and finish uses the shared building demolition path. Existing fixed spawn and transit protection and the terrain edit budget remain in effect. Nearby standing players, including the thrower, receive an outward and upward movement impulse within 240 units, with distance falloff. Ordinary movement collision prevents the impulse from teleporting them through walls. Dynamite itself applies no health damage.

The host owns projectile physics, fuses, destruction and impulses. Guests receive charges and blast events in motion snapshots; removed construction and excavated terrain use durable world replication and saving. Building permission is required to throw a charge and is rechecked for destruction at detonation. Up to eight charges can be active, with a 750 ms cooldown per player. Holding the fire button does not repeatedly throw.

A construction blast is one demolition transaction and one construction undo entry. Dependent crane parts are removed with their supports; loaded cranes release cargo, and broken railway updates the player train. Refunds follow the demolition economy. Construction Undo restores the removed structures; excavated terrain uses normal persistent terrain edits. Charges, held state, and blast animations clear when restoring a world.

Audio uses a real explosive-device detonation recording, **Explosive** by qubodup, sourced from US government footage according to the uploader, plus **Simple Fuse Sound** by themightyglider, derived from j1987's recorded firework fuse. Both are CC0/public domain. The explosion asset contains only the third and final blast from the source recording, including its decay, and is played at its original pitch. The fuse loops only for its remaining burn time and stops when the charge disappears. Sources and download links are in `public/audio/friends/dynamite-sources.md`.

`FriendsDynamite.test.ts` checks throwing, collisions, cooldown, limits, shared timing, replication, saving, all terrain materials, permissions, impulses, crane dependencies and train removal. Held-item tests check palm placement, desktop and portrait framing, release and refill. Visual tests check finite debris transforms, one explosion sound, fuse cleanup and effect disposal. `tools/test-friends-dynamite.mjs` exercises actual Fun-bar selection, visible held dynamite, production throw input, terrain/structure destruction and the player impulse in Chromium. `tools/friends-dynamite-review.html` provides a replayable visual and audio preview.
