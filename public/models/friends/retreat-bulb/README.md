# Stillwater hanging bulb

[Lightbulb 01 by Josh Dean](https://polyhaven.com/a/lightbulb_01), downloaded
9 October 2026 from Poly Haven's official files API. CC0-1.0; see
https://polyhaven.com/license. `manifest.json` records source files, official
URLs and provider MD5 hashes. All downloaded files match those hashes.

The source glTF, buffer and 1K PBR maps are unmodified. The renderer flips the
bulb to hang cap-up, gives the filament a warm switched emissive response, and
uses lightweight transparent glass in place of screen-space transmission. The
black suspension cord, canopy and socket mount are authored room geometry.
No download occurs during play.
